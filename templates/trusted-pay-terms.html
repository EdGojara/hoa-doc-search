These terms apply when you use Trusted Pay, the online payment service in your homeowner portal, to pay amounts you owe to your homeowners association (the "Association"). Trusted Pay is offered by Bedrock Association Management ("Bedrock", "we", "us"), which manages the Association. Please read these terms before you pay.

1. Accepting these terms

Before any payment is submitted, you will be asked to confirm that you have reviewed the payment amount, the Payment Processing Fee and these terms. A payment is only submitted after you give that confirmation. If you do not agree to these terms, do not submit a payment; you may still pay the Association by any other method it accepts.

2. One-time payment authorization

When you submit a payment, you authorize a single charge to the card or bank account you provide, for the Total Payment shown on the review screen. That total is the payment to the Association plus any Payment Processing Fee. Each payment is a separate, one-time authorization.

3. Bank account (ACH) payments

If you pay from a bank account, you authorize a one-time electronic debit (ACH) from that account for the Total Payment, and, if a debit is made in error, a correcting credit or debit for the amount of the error. You confirm that you are the account holder or are authorized to use the account, and that it is a checking or savings account in the United States. Bank payments usually take several business days to complete. A payment is not considered complete until the funds have actually been received.

4. Automatic (recurring) payments

Automatic payments are not currently available through Trusted Pay. If they are offered in the future, they will require a separate authorization that states the amount or how it is calculated, the timing, how you will be notified before each payment, and how to cancel. Nothing in these terms authorizes a recurring payment.

5. Payment methods, limits and right to decline

Trusted Pay may accept bank account (ACH) payments and credit or debit card payments. The methods available to you are shown when you pay. We or our payment processors may set limits on the amount or number of payments, and may decline or reverse a payment that exceeds those limits, cannot be verified, or is refused by a payment processor or financial institution.

6. Payment Processing Fee

Some payment methods include a Payment Processing Fee. Before you authorize a payment, the review screen shows, as exact dollar amounts:

  • the Assessment/Payment Amount going to the Association,
  • the Payment Processing Fee, and
  • the Total Payment you are authorizing.

The fee can differ by payment method and may be a flat amount, a percentage of the payment, or both. The fee is not part of your Association balance and is not paid to the Association. You can cancel at any time before you submit the payment, and no fee is charged unless a payment is submitted. Once a payment has been processed, the Payment Processing Fee is generally not refundable, except where the law requires a refund or a written refund policy we publish provides one.

7. Returned or failed payments

If a payment is returned, reversed, disputed or does not complete for any reason (for example, insufficient funds or a closed account), the amount is treated as unpaid and your obligation to the Association continues. The Association may apply charges allowed by its governing documents and applicable law for a returned payment. We will make reasonable efforts to notify you when a payment fails.

Please review your receipts and account statements. If you believe a payment was unauthorized or made in error, contact us promptly at {{SUPPORT_REACH}}, and include the property address, the payment date and the amount. You may also have rights under federal law and the rules of your card issuer or bank, including the right to dispute a charge; these terms do not limit those rights.

9. Third-party payment processing

Bedrock is not a bank and does not itself process card or bank payments. Payments are processed by third-party payment processors and financial institutions selected by Bedrock. Your payment information is sent directly to those processors to complete the payment. Their handling of a payment may also be subject to their own terms.

10. Privacy of payment information

We use your payment information only to process your payment, keep payment and account records for the Association, prevent fraud, and meet legal requirements. We share it only with the payment processors, financial institutions and service providers needed for those purposes, and with the Association to the extent needed to credit your account. We do not sell your payment information. Full card numbers are handled by our payment processors and are not stored by Bedrock.

11. Suspension and decline

You can still pay the Association by other accepted methods.
