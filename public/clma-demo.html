Ask Amanda — safe live questions

She cites CLMA's own bylaws and maintenance agreement on these. Ask two, and show the citation each time:

  • What is the quorum for a board meeting under CLMA's bylaws?
  • What areas is CLMA responsible for maintaining under the maintenance agreement?
  • How can the bylaws be amended?  /  What is the collection policy for delinquent accounts?

Then ask one she cannot answer, on purpose:

  • "What is our irrigation variance year to date?" → she says she does not have it in the records available and offers to flag it for the team. "That answer matters to me. One thing we have spent real time on is teaching these agents when not to answer."

Before the room

  • Pre-stage the bid review that morning so you click into it, not wait on email live.
  • Open the asset map in Chrome and confirm the satellite tiles load.
  • Do not show another community's real data. Name Canyon Gate at Cinco Ranch and Waterview verbally as area proof.
  • Do not tour every feature. Two map pins, one bid review, two or three Amanda questions.
