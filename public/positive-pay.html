🛡️ Positive Pay — NewFirst National Bank

On any day trustEd issues checks on a NewFirst account, Kat prepares one file of every check the bank has not yet confirmed (new checks, and any carried forward from a failed upload), uploads it in NewFirst's portal, and records the bank's confirmation here. No checks, no file. NewFirst matches presented checks against it and emails us any exception; Kat investigates and an authorized person decides pay or return in the bank portal.
