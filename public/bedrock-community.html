🤝 In the Community

Ways to get Bedrock woven into the local community over the next 12–16 months — building relationships, supporting good causes, and letting people see what we’re creating, without it feeling like sales. Relationships first, business second. Nothing here is firm yet; it’s a board for the team to react to and pick from.

How we’re approaching it

  • Consistency over big checks — small, regular participation beats one large event.
  • Relationships first — time, organization, and showing up, not logo placement.
  • Tie events to a cause — every gathering has a community purpose behind it.
  • Co-sponsor with vendors — partners help with cost so we’re not paying for everything.
  • Let the technology be the “wow” — show real work, don’t pitch AI in the abstract.
  • Document everything — a small project has big impact if we tell the story well.
