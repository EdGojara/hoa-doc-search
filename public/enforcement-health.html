What each column means

Dup risk — pending observation whose case already had its notice mailed. Confirming it would send a duplicate.
Phantom (void) — violation whose category no longer matches its observation, and the corrected category already has a case. Safe to void.
Phantom (recat) — same mismatch, but the corrected category has no case yet. Needs re-pointing, not voiding.
Rejected still open — violation whose observation was rejected but the case was never voided.
Orphan drafts — a draft letter sitting on a voided or cured violation. Should never mail.
Open, no letter — open courtesy_1 with no letter at all. Either a lost render or a case that needs a first notice.
