Landscape Maintenance RFP — Bid Comparison

Cinco Landscape Maintenance Association · 3 bids received · scope normalized onto the same lines by trustEd

Recommendation

TerraCare Grounds is the recommended finalist: it covers the full acreage including irrigation responsibility, carries the required insurance, and its all-in annual cost is competitive once the excluded items in the other bids are added back. GreenSpan looks cheapest on the base fee but excludes irrigation and carries a 6% annual escalation, so its true multi-year cost is highest.
