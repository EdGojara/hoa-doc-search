Onboarding Engine

Bring a community in from a legacy system. Trusted runs the routine stages on its own and stops only when it needs a missing report, your judgment on an exception, or your authorization. The legacy books at the cutoff are the authority. A failed control stays failed; only the owner can waive it, with a reason. Nothing here writes to the community's books.
