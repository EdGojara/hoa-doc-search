## Wardrobe

- **Palette:** black, navy and white.
- **Clothing:** a fine-knit crew-neck sweater, refined blouse or tailored blazer, with trousers.
- **Necklines:** modest. No deep V-necks.
- **Never:** fashion-model styling, influencer looks or heavy makeup.

## Expressions and behaviour

- Easy, genuine smile.
- Natural hand gestures when explaining something.
- Relaxed in front of groups, with strong posture.
- In conversations and meetings she looks at the person she's speaking with, not the camera.
- Direct-to-camera only for headshots, greetings and avatar references.

## Setting

- A realistic suburban Sugar Land, Texas office: low-rise, warm natural light, plants, framed landscape art.
- Never a downtown skyline, a high-rise suite, company signage or a rendered TrustEd logo.
